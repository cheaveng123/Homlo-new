## 2. Information we collect

### Information you give us

- Account details: name, email address, phone number, password (stored securely, never in plain text).
- Employer details: business name, contact person, location, business description.
- Job seeker details: profile information, work history, CV or resume, and any message or application you send.
- Listings and content you post.
- Support messages and reports you send us.
- Confirmations you give, such as your acceptance of our Terms and your 18+ confirmation, with the date and time.

### Information collected automatically

- Device and usage data: IP address, browser type, device identifiers, pages viewed, and actions on the Platform.
- Cookies and similar technologies (see section 8).

### Payment information

Payments are processed by our payment provider [PROVIDER NAME]. We receive limited details such as payment status, amount, and the last four digits of a card. We do not store your full card number.

### Information from others

If you sign in with a third-party account (such as Google or Facebook), we receive the profile details you allow. Users may also report information about other users.

## 3. How we use your information

- To create and manage your account and provide the Platform.
- To show listings, enable applications, and let users contact each other.
- To process payments, boosts and subscriptions and send receipts.
- To verify your email and phone number and to prevent fraud, scams, spam and abuse.
- To review reports, enforce our Terms, and remove prohibited content or accounts.
- To keep records of consent and moderation decisions to protect our legal rights.
- To communicate with you about your account, security, and service updates.
- To send marketing messages, only where you have agreed or the law allows, with an easy way to opt out.
- To improve and secure the Platform and analyze usage.
- To comply with legal obligations.

## 4. Legal bases (where required, for example under GDPR)

We rely on: performing our contract with you; our legitimate interests (running a safe platform, preventing fraud, improving the service); your consent (such as marketing messages or non-essential cookies); and legal obligations.

## 5. Who we share information with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only:

- With other users, as part of how the Platform works. Employer details in a listing are public. A job seeker's application and profile details are shared with the employer you apply to.
- With service providers that help us run the Platform, such as hosting, email and SMS, analytics, payment processing, customer support, and identity or fraud-prevention services (such as [VERIFICATION PROVIDER], if we use one), under agreements that limit how they use your data.
- For legal reasons, to comply with law, court orders or valid requests from authorities, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of users, the public, or us.
- In a business transfer, such as a merger or sale of our business, with notice to you where required.
